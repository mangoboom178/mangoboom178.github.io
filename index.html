Questions

Does it block websites?+

No. Nothing is ever blocked. He notices, says something, and writes you a citation if you keep going. Close the tab or press “This is work” and he backs off.

Does it record my screen?+

No. It reads the title of the window in front and, in browsers, the site's address. That stays on your PC. No screenshots, no keystrokes, nothing uploaded.

What if I'm on a call or playing a game?+

During calls he only shows subtitles. Presentations and full-screen games: he stays out of the picture.

Can he be quieter?+

Pick Soft, Normal or Hardcore, or set your own minutes for each step. Turn the voice off for subtitles only.

Is there a Mac version?+

Not yet. TabCop is for Windows 10 and 11.

How do I cancel?+

Online, any time, from the link in your receipt email. You keep TabCop until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get my money back?+

Yes: within 14 days of your first payment, or of a yearly renewal, you get a full refund. No questions.
