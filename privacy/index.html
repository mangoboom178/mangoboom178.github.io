Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026.

The short version

The TabCop app works on your PC. What it watches stays on your PC. We only learn what you tell us when you buy a licence or join the launch list.

What the app reads, and where it stays

  • The title of the window in front of you and the name of its program.
  • In supported browsers, the address of the active tab, to tell sites apart.
  • During setup and only if you tick the box: your browser history from the last 30 days, read once to suggest rules.

From these the app keeps your rules, your citations and daily totals in a file on your PC. Statistics older than 90 days are deleted. None of this is sent anywhere. The app does not take screenshots, record keystrokes or read page contents.

Crash reports

Off by default. If you turn them on, crash reports go to Sentry with window titles, addresses, program names, history and file paths removed, and without your IP address being stored.

Payments

Lemon Squeezy sells TabCop licences as the merchant of record. They process your payment details and billing address; we receive your email, your licence key and what you bought. The app checks your licence key with Lemon Squeezy once a day.

Launch list

If you join it, Buttondown stores your email. We use it to tell you when TabCop is out. Every email has an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing deletes your address from the list. We never sell or share it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you (your email, licence and purchase), or ask us to delete it. Data on your PC is yours: uninstalling TabCop and deleting its folder removes it.
